Privacy Policy — BloxStory AR Uploader

Last updated: 23 November 2026

"BloxStory AR Uploader" is a private desktop tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel BloxStory AR (@BloxStoryAR). It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's explicit consent through Google OAuth, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's own videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publishing schedule, and manage the channel's playlists.

Data handling

  • No data about other users or viewers is collected, stored or shared.
  • The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared with third parties.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking is performed by the tool.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube's own handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Through the channel's YouTube page: youtube.com/@BloxStoryAR.
